There is a point in every home-lab project where the original requirement quietly leaves the room.

The requirement here was simple: show a few time zones on a small display. That should have been an afternoon. Instead I started thinking about daylight saving time, meeting overlap, sunrise, visual density, and whether Alexandria deserved a tiny weather glyph.

## The “while I’m here” tax

The project accumulated features one perfectly reasonable decision at a time:

1. Multiple cities, because one clock would be boring.
2. Color-coded working hours, because time without context is not very useful.
3. A responsive layout, because I wanted it on more than one screen.
4. Keyboard controls, because now it had become a *tool*.

Nothing was individually excessive. Together, they were a weekend.

## I would still do it again

Tiny home-lab projects are a good place to practice taste. There is no roadmap and no stakeholder meeting. You get to decide which detail matters—and when to stop.

I am still working on that last part.
