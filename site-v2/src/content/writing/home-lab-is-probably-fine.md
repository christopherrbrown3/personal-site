The home lab currently contains several small computers, an unnecessary number of cables, and one service whose purpose I understand only when it stops working.

This is, broadly, the point.

Production systems are designed to hide a lot of sharp edges. A lab lets you pick them up carefully. You can break DNS, misunderstand a container network, and discover why backups are not the same thing as restoration—all before lunch.

The lights are blinking. The dashboards are green. It is probably fine.
