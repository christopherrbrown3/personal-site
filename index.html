How I lead / 03

The operating model behind the work.

Good leadership is a repeatable system: see the whole environment, make the decision legible, build mechanisms that reduce heroics, and transfer ownership to the people closest to the work.

“AI should remove operational drag—not human judgment.”
  1. 01

    Read the system

    Start with customer stakes, business context, technical signals, and organizational constraints—not a preferred tool.

  2. 02

    Frame the decision

    Translate technical complexity into choices, owners, tradeoffs, and a shared executive narrative.

  3. 03

    Build the mechanism

    Turn the decision into operating cadence, tooling, quality controls, and feedback loops that can scale.

  4. 04

    Grow the owners

    Coach people into greater scope, make expectations explicit, and build succession before it becomes urgent.
