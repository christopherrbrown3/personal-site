Selected work

A few things I’m proud of.

Good work should be easy to explain. Here are three examples.

01

Made incident reviews more useful.

I led a joint effort between AWS and a global technology company to improve how teams learned from major incidents. We brought leaders together, clarified how reviews should work, and turned the lessons into everyday practice.

The result: faster reviews, clearer ownership, and a stronger support model across regions.

02

Built AI tools people actually use.

I helped create tools that summarize support cases, surface important issues, and make recurring reporting faster. I kept people in control when customer impact and judgment mattered most.

The result: tools used by thousands of employees, plus practical AI training for hundreds more.

03

Helped a team grow from 7 to 30+.

I helped grow a team of Technical Account Managers from 7 to more than 30. At the same time, I made promotion expectations clearer for people who wanted to grow as technical experts or managers.

The result: stronger paths to promotion, better succession planning, and the team’s first Principal-level specialist.
